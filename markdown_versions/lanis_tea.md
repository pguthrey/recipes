
# Lani's Tea

Once time I was in San Antonio for Chrismas. We were having drinks on the picturesque riverwalk at Domingo's, and I asked our mixologist Lani to make me a "Long island iced tea, but like for San Antonio - a San Antonio Iced Tea".

It was delicious and crisp. We were drinking it the whole trip. 

- 1/2 oz vodka
- 1/2 oz tequila
- 1/2 oz gin
- 1/2 oz lemon juice
- 1/2 oz lime juice
- 3/4 oz triple sec
- 1/2 oz agave (3/4 oz simple)
- 1 inch cucumber, muddled
- 1 oz coke to finish

Muddle the cucumber in the glass and pour over the shaken mixture.
