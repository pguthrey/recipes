
# Fast Food Sauces

## In & Out

- 1 part mayo
- 1 part ketchup
- diced pickle

## Raising Cane's

- 1/2 cup mayo
- 1/4 cup ketchup
- 1 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp worchestershire sauce

## Red Robin's Campire sauce

- 1/2 cup mayo
- 1/2 cup hickory BBQ sauce
- 1 tsp dijon
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 dash worchestershire
- pinch chili powder
