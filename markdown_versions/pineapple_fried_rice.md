
## Pineapple Fried Rice

For about 6 servings:

- 6 cups cooked, chilled sushi rice
- 2 cups fresh pineapple chunks
- 3 cups frozen mixed vegetables
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 5 cloves garlic, minced
- 5 green onions, sliced
- 3 - 4 Tbsp neutral high-heat oil
- MSG

## Cook rice

- Cook the rice, but fill the water to just slightly below the rice cooker line rather than exactly to it. Don’t dramatically reduce the water, just a small percent less.
- Once cooked, fluff it immediately. 
- Spread it in a thin layer on a sheet pan or large tray.
- Let steam escape for 20–30 minutes.
- Refrigerate uncovered until cold, then loosely cover it.
- Ideally use it the next day.

## Stir fry sauce

- 1/3 cup low-sodium soy sauce
- 2 Tbsp hoisin sauce
- 2 Tbsp rice vinegar
- 2 Tbsp brown sugar or maple syrup
- 1 Tbsp toasted sesame oil
- 2 tsp fresh grated ginger
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1/2-1 tsp MSG, optional

Mix everything beforehand. Start with about 2/3 of this sauce, then add more after tasting.

## Rice prep

- Before frying, break the cold rice apart with your hands. Sushi rice will still form some clusters. You don't need every grain completely separate.

## Griddle Pineapple

- Heat your griddle to about 400–425°F.
- Add a bit of oil 
- Spread the pineapple into a single layer.
- Cook without moving it for 2–3 minutes, then turn it and cook another 2 minutes.
- We want each side to carmelize nicely and maybe even char a little bit
- Move it to a cool area afterward.

## Griddle Vegetables

- Cook the onion and frozen vegetables with about 1 Tbsp oil for 4–6 minutes.
- Ensure the steam from the frozen vegetables fully cooks off.
- Add garlic for the final 30–60 seconds.
- Move aside to a warm area, ensure there is a large area for the rice

## Griddle rice

- Add 2 Tbsp oil to the hot griddle.
- Fry the sushi rice hard
- Spread the cold rice relatively thin.
- Leave the rice undisturbed, for about 90 seconds.
- Let the bottom make direct contact with the griddle and develop some crust.
- Flip sections of rice with your spatulas rather than constantly stirring.
-  Cook another 3–5 minutes, allowing the rice to fry between turns.
- With sushi rice, you're unlikely to get the completely separated grains of restaurant-style jasmine-rice fried rice. Instead, you're aiming for small toasted clusters with crispy edges, which can actually be excellent.

## Add the sauce

- Combine the rice and vegetables.
- Drizzle about 2/3 of the sauce broadly over everything while tossing.
- Cook 1-2 minutes.
- Taste. Add more sauce only if necessary.

## Finish 

- Fold in the pineapple
- Sprinkle sesame seeds
- Cook another 1–2 minutes.
- Garnish with with green onions
