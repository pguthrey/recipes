I have tried to make slab bacon dishes many times, and they have previously been a hit at parties, but they still had a weird too-chewy texture that I have been seeking to eliminate. The answer was a two parter - blanching and pressure cooking. 

## Blanching 

Bring water to a boil, then drop the pork in.
Boil the pork for 3 - 5 minutes, then rinse clean with cold water.

On the internet, some say to add ginger, green onion, Sichuan peppercorn, and sake to the boiling water before blanching.
