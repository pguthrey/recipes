## Mango Habanero Chicken Wings with Curry Yogurt Dip

## Ingredients 

- 3 lbs chicken wings
- Lime
- 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt
- Salsa for flavor (1 jar)
- Tomato Sauce (small can)
- Soy sauce
- Honey
- Cider Vinegar
- Ground Ginger

## Cook chicken

- Sous vide the chicken wings at 165F for 2 hrs
- Optional - In an air fryer or oven, Broil the chicken until crispy (2-3 mins).

## Make spicy Sauce

Combine:
- 1 cup of the salsa (1/2 jar)
- 1/2 cup tomato sauce (small can)
- 1/3 cup honey
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 2 tablespoons cider vinegar
- Juice of 1 lime
- 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger

Over low heat, simmer, stirring occasionally, until thickened and reduced.

## Make the dip

Combine:
- 1 cup Salsa (1/2 jar)
- 1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 2 tablespoons mayonnaise
- 1 tablespoon honey
- 1/2 teaspoon curry powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt

Refrigerate.

## Finish 

- Using tongs, dip chicken in mango habanero sauce until completely coated. 
- Arrange chicken back onto wire rack in a single layer and broil for another 3 to 5 minutes or until sticky and nicely charred. Serve hot.
