Plant based Hawaiian Macaroni Salad

Ingredients

  • 1 lb elbow macaroni
  • 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 2 cups Best Foods Plant Based Mayo
  • 1/3 cup unsweetened plain almond milk, plus more as needed
  • 1 medium carrot, finely grated
  • 1/3 cup yellow or sweet onion, finely grated or minced
  • 2 green onions, thinly sliced

Prep

Grate and mix: - 1 medium carrot, finely grated - 1/3 cup yellow or sweet onion, finely grated or minced - 2 green onions, thinly sliced

Instructions

Boil the macaroni for around 9 minutes, then let sit for 10-15 minutes. While pasta is sitting, work on the dressing.

Cook the macaroni

Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil. Cook the macaroni about 1–2 minutes past al dente - Hawaiian macaroni salad is traditionally quite soft. Drain well. Do not rinse. Immediately toss the hot macaroni with 2 tbsp apple cider vinegar. Let it sit for about 10–15 minutes so the vinegar absorbs into the pasta.

Make the dressing

Whisk together: - 2 cups plant-based mayo - 1/3 cup almond milk - 1 tbsp sugar - 1 tsp salt - 1/2 tsp black pepper

Mix it all up

While the pasta is still slightly warm, stir in the grated vegetables into the macaroni, followed by 3/4 of the dressing. Reserve the remaining dressing. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 - 3 hours. Overnight is even better.

Finish

Stir in the reserved dressing. If it seems too thick or dry, add almond milk 1 tbsp at a time until creamy. Make it very creamy. The macaroni absorbs a substantial amount of dressing as it chills. Taste and adjust salt, pepper, or sugar.
