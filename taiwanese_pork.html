Tawianese Fast - Braised Pork Belly

I have tried to make slab bacon dishes many times, and they have previously been a hit at parties, but they still had a weird too-chewy texture that I have been seeking to eliminate. The answer was a two parter - blanching and pressure cooking.

Ingredient

  • 2.5 lb Pork Belly
  • 4 thin slices of Ginger
  • 10 cloves Garlic smashed
  • 2 Green Onion
  • 1 Cinamon Stick
  • 4 Star Anise
  • 5 Red Whole Chili
  • 1/4 cup Soy Sauce
  • 1/4 cup Dark Soy Sauce
  • 4.5 tbsp Brown Sugar or Rock Sugar
  • 3 tbsp Chinese Cooking Wine (optional)
  • 1/4 cup Water

Prep

  • Cut the pork belly into 1 - inch pieces.

  • Reserve some green onion for garnish.

  • Add all other ingredients to an Instapot.

Blanching

  • We will add the pork to cold water in a large pot. Then, bring water to a boil. then drop the pork in.

  • Boil the pork for 3 - 5 minutes, then rinse clean with cold water.

  • Some online say to add ginger, green onion, Sichuan peppercorn, and sake to the boiling water before blanching.

Cook

  • Mix all of the sauce ingredients. Then add the pork.
  • Pressure cook for 25 minutes on high pressure.
  • Pair with rice and garnish with more green onion.
